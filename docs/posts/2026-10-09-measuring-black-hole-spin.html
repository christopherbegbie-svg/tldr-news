Measuring Black Hole Spin

How stars reveal cosmic secrets about Sagittarius A*

  • Sagittarius A* is a supermassive black hole at our galaxy's center
  • Black holes spin and rotate just like stars and planets
  • Scientists track nearby stars to measure the black hole's rotation speed
  • A star's orbit reveals the black hole's gravitational influence and spin
  • This helps us understand how galaxies form and evolve over time
