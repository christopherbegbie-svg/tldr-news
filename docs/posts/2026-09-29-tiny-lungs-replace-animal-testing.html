Tiny Lungs Replace Animal Testing

USB-sized organs could revolutionize drug development forever

  • Scientists created a human lung smaller than a USB stick
  • It breathes, expands & contracts like a real lung
  • Made of polymer channels lined with human lung cells
  • Could replace millions of lab animals in drug testing
  • Gives pharma companies actual human data, not rat data
