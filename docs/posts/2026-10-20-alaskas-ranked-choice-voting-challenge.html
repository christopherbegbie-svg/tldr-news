Alaska's Ranked-Choice Voting Challenge

How voting systems shape real election outcomes

  • Alaska uses ranked-choice voting, not traditional voting
  • Voters rank candidates by preference (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
  • If no candidate gets 50%, lowest votes get redistributed
  • Same-name candidates can split votes unexpectedly
  • Election rules shape outcomes as much as voters do
