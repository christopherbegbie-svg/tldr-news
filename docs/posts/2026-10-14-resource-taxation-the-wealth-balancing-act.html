Resource Taxation: The Wealth Balancing Act

Why governments struggle to tax natural resources fairly

  • Australia exports billions in gas but collects relatively little tax revenue
  • Resources like gas, oil, and minerals belong to the public but are extracted by private companies
  • Tax rates on resources affect both company profits and government funding for services
  • Low taxes = more corporate profit but less money for schools and healthcare
  • High taxes = better public services but companies may relocate elsewhere
