Ceasefires: pauses that save lives

How temporary truces help civilians escape conflict

  • A ceasefire is a temporary halt in fighting between warring parties
  • Usually brokered by neutral third parties like the United Nations or individual nations
  • During ceasefires, humanitarian aid reaches trapped civilians
  • They create escape windows for people fleeing conflict zones
  • Ceasefires aren't permanent peace—fighting can resume when they end
