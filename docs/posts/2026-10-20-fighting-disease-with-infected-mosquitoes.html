Fighting Disease With Infected Mosquitoes

How Barcelona used nature to fight dengue transmission

  • Male mosquitoes infected with a parasite can't spread dengue
  • When they mate with wild females, they pass on the parasite
  • This reduces disease-carrying mosquitoes in the population naturally
  • Barcelona released them for the first time in Europe
  • It's biological control—safer than pesticides, smarter than spraying
