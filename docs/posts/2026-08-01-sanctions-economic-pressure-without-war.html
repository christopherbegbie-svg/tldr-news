Sanctions: Economic Pressure Without War

How countries punish each other politically and economically.

  • Sanctions are economic penalties one country uses to pressure another without military force
  • They include freezing assets, banning trade, and blocking technology access
  • A bipartisan bill seeks to increase sanctions on Russia—showing rare political unity
  • But concerns about presidential tariff power are slowing its progress
  • This reveals how competing interests in government can complicate foreign policy decisions
