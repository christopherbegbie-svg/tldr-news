Yellowstone's Wolf Comeback Story

How eliminating predators changes everything, and why they were brought back.

  • Wolves were completely eliminated from Yellowstone by 1930
  • Biologists reintroduced them in 1995—nearly 70 years later
  • They're now called cultural icons and ecosystem engineers
  • Wolves control deer populations, protecting entire landscapes
  • Yellowstone is now one of the world's best places to see wild wolves
