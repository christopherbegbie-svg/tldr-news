Dinosaur Poop Reveals Bird Extinction Survival

A fossilized coprolite holds clues to evolution's greatest mystery

  • A cherry-sized fossil of dinosaur poop was found in Montana in 2016
  • Inside it: feathers, bones, and evidence of a bird's final meal
  • A theropod dinosaur ate the bird before it became fossilized
  • This gives us rare insight into what dinosaurs actually hunted
  • Reveals clues about how some birds survived the mass extinction
