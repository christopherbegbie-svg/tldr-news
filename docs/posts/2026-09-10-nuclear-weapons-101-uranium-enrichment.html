Nuclear Weapons 101: Uranium Enrichment

Why centrifuges matter in global security threats

  • Uranium enrichment separates heavy and light uranium atoms using centrifuges

  • North Korea built a new facility with 28 centrifuge cascades at Yongbyon
  • The IAEA monitors nuclear programs to prevent weapons proliferation
  • Enrichment is the hardest technical barrier to building nuclear bombs
