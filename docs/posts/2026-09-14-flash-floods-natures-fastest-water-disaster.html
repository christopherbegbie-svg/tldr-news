Flash Floods: Nature's Fastest Water Disaster

How heavy rain becomes a life-threatening emergency in minutes

  • Flash floods happen when heavy rain overwhelms drainage faster than water escapes
  • Water can rise several feet in MINUTES, not hours
  • Concrete and pavement prevent soil from absorbing water, making floods worse
  • Moving water has incredible force—it can sweep away cars and people
  • Early warning systems and emergency planning save lives during these events
