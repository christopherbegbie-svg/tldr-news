Why a neutral nation built 370,000 shelters

  • Switzerland is officially neutral but built the world's largest bunker network
  • Over 370,000 nuclear shelters exist across the country
  • Built during the Cold War for protection from nuclear attacks
  • Most countries abandoned bunkers after 1989—Switzerland kept building
  • Today, some bunkers house refugees and migrants
