When Covid Wakes Sleeping Viruses

How dormant viruses in your body affect illness severity

  • Most people carry dormant viruses like herpesvirus in their bodies
  • These viruses normally stay 'asleep' and cause no harm
  • Covid-19 infection may trigger these sleeping viruses to reactivate
  • Reactivation can worsen Covid symptoms and increase long Covid risk
  • Understanding this helps explain why illness severity varies between people
