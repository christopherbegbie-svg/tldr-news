Fish Follow One Influential Neighbor

How animals make group decisions using virtual reality science

  • Scientists studied rummy-nose tetra fish using virtual reality
  • Each fish focused on copying just ONE neighbor's movements
  • They ignored all other fish in the group
  • This shows how leaders emerge in animal societies
  • Understanding group behavior helps predict real-world crowd dynamics
