Seaweed farming and climate change myths

Why ocean solutions need preservation, not just innovation

  • Scientists hoped seaweed farming could remove hundreds of millions of tonnes of CO2
  • Seaweed absorbs CO2 while growing—a key part of the ocean carbon cycle
  • But most carbon is released back when seaweed decays in the ocean
  • Warming waters are killing kelp forests faster than we can farm them
  • The real solution? Protect existing ecosystems, not just create new ones
