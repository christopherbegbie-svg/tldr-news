Maps Don't Tell the Whole Truth

How a 400-year-old projection distorted global perspective

  • The Mercator map (1569) stretches polar regions and shrinks equatorial ones
  • Africa is actually 11.7 million sq miles—14x larger than most maps show
  • Europe appears 3.5x bigger than it actually is on Mercator projections
  • Maps aren't neutral—they reflect power and shape how we see the world
  • The UN now backs more accurate projections to correct 400 years of distortion
