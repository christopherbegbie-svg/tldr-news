Treasury Yields Hit 5%: What It Means

Understanding how markets signal inflation and Fed policy

  • Treasury yields are interest rates the government pays to borrow money
  • Higher yields signal inflation concerns in the market
  • This affects mortgage rates, car loans, and business borrowing
  • The Federal Reserve watches yields to decide on interest rate policy
  • Your financial decisions are influenced by these invisible market signals
