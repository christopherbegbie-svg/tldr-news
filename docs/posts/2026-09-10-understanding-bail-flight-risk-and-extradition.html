Understanding Bail, Flight Risk, and Extradition

How courts decide who stays free while facing international justice

  • Extradition is when one country legally transfers someone to another to face trial
  • Bail gets denied when courts believe someone is a "flight risk"—likely to flee
  • Prosecutors use evidence like multiple passports and private jets to prove flight risk
  • Defense lawyers sometimes argue such behavior was "playing a role" for audiences
  • Courts must balance individual freedom with ensuring people face their legal obligations
