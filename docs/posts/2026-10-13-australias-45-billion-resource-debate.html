Australia's $45 Billion Resource Debate

Should governments tax natural wealth more aggressively?

  • Australia may be undercharging for natural gas & minerals
  • That missing revenue could fund healthcare, rent assistance & food security
  • Governments face a tough choice: tax resources heavily OR keep taxes low to attract investment
  • Higher taxation = more public funding but potentially fewer companies willing to operate there
  • Lower taxation = more business investment but less revenue for citizens
