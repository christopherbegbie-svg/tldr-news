50,000-Year-Old Paintings Under Wildfire Threat

Indonesia's ancient cave art faces unprecedented danger from climate crisis

  • The Maros-Pangkep caves hold Earth's oldest known figurative art—51,000+ years old
  • Hand stencils and animal paintings created by our earliest ancestors
  • Wildfires now threaten this UNESCO Global Geopark and irreplaceable cultural heritage
  • These paintings predate famous European cave art by thousands of years
  • Losing them means losing direct evidence of how humans first expressed creativity
