Yemen's Hidden Conflict Explained

How proxy wars reshape nations and harm millions

  • Yemen is split between two forces: Saudi-backed government and Iran-backed Houthis
  • Both sides fight for territory and control of key cities like Taiz
  • Supply routes are being cut off, starving civilians trapped in besieged areas
  • This is a "proxy war"—larger powers (Saudi Arabia & Iran) fight through smaller forces
  • Millions of Yemenis face humanitarian crisis as a result
