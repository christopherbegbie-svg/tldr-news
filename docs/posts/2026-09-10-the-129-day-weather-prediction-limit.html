The 129-Day Weather Prediction Limit

Why chaos theory stops meteorologists cold

  • Weather can only be reliably predicted ~129 days ahead (about 4 months)
  • Tiny measurement errors grow exponentially over time
  • This is called the "butterfly effect"—small changes cascade into big differences
  • Chaos theory explains why perfect prediction is impossible
  • This applies to climate and weather forecasting at every scale
