Old Crimes, New Trials: What's Legal?

Understanding statutes of limitation and government power

  • The Trump administration is attempting to prosecute former FBI Director James Comey for an alleged classified leak from a decade ago
  • Comey's legal team says this violates the statute of limitations—a law that sets time limits on prosecutions
  • The statute of limitations protects citizens from being prosecuted after too much time has passed
  • This case raises questions about whether prosecutions can be politically motivated
  • Understanding legal protections like this helps you recognize how democracies protect individual rights
