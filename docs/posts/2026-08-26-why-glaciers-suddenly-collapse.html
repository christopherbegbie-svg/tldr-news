Why Glaciers Suddenly Collapse

Melting snow weakens massive ice, triggering catastrophic mountain disasters

  • A glacier at 5km altitude collapsed in Nepal's mountain valley
  • Melting snow weakened the ice structure over time
  • The glacier plunged about 1 kilometre in a single collapse
  • This triggered catastrophic floods and dangerous avalanches
  • Rising global temperatures are making these events more common
