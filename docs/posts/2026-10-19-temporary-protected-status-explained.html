Temporary Protected Status Explained

Why 100,000 migrants got more time to stay

  • 100,000+ Ukrainians and Sudanese migrants received a deportation reprieve
  • Their Temporary Protected Status (TPS) was set to expire Monday
  • TPS is legal protection when countries face war, disaster, or crisis
  • It allows migrants to live, work, and stay temporarily while conditions improve
  • DHS extended their protections—keeping families intact and economic contributors in place
