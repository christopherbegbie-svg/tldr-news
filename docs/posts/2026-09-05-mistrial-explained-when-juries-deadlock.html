Mistrial explained: When juries deadlock

Why unanimous verdicts matter in criminal justice

  • A mistrial happens when jurors can't unanimously agree on a verdict
  • The jury becomes "hung"—too divided to convict or acquit
  • The entire trial is essentially erased and starts over
  • It protects defendants but delays justice for all parties
  • High-profile cases often attract media attention when this occurs
