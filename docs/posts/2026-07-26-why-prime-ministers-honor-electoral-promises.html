Why Prime Ministers Honor Electoral Promises

The rules that keep democracy accountable and fair

  • Voters elect leaders to fulfill a manifesto (promised plan)
  • Breaking that promise by calling early elections damages democratic trust
  • Terms exist so governments must deliver on their commitments
  • Calling unnecessary elections wastes resources and time
  • This balance keeps leaders accountable to voters, not just themselves
