Thermal Imaging Unlocks Better Gravity Detectors

How fixing heat distortions reveals cosmic secrets

  • Gravitational-wave detectors sense ripples in spacetime from cosmic collisions
  • Heat distorts the mirrors, creating noise that blocks detection
  • Thermal imaging monitors and corrects these distortions in real-time
  • Higher sensitivity = detecting more distant and subtle cosmic events
  • This breakthrough unlocks deeper understanding of black holes and neutron stars
