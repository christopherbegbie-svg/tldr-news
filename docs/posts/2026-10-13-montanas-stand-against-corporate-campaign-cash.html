Montana's Stand Against Corporate Campaign Cash

How voters could limit unlimited political spending

  • Citizens United ruling (2010) allowed unlimited corporate political spending
  • Anonymous corporate money has flooded elections nationwide since then
  • Montana voters now have a ballot measure to restrict corporate spending in state politics
  • This is a direct challenge to the Supreme Court's interpretation of free speech
  • The outcome could influence how other states regulate campaign finance
