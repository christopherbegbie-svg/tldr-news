Ancient mammals had regrowable reptile-like teeth

Fossil discovery reshapes our understanding of evolution

  • This Cretaceous fossil mammal could replace its teeth throughout life
  • Modern mammals only get 2 sets of teeth total
  • This ability is similar to how reptiles constantly grow new teeth
  • Found in China, it lived ~100 million years ago
  • Shows our ancestors had evolutionary abilities we've since lost
