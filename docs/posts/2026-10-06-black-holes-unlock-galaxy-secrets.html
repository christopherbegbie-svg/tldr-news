Black Holes Unlock Galaxy Secrets

Why impossibly fast stars reveal cosmic truths

  • Thousands of stars orbit Sagittarius A*, a supermassive black hole at our galaxy's heart
  • These stars move at extraordinary speeds—thousands of kilometers per second
  • Scientists use their orbits to measure the black hole's mass and gravity
  • This proves Einstein's theory of gravity works even in extreme conditions
  • Understanding these stellar dynamics reveals how galaxies form and evolve
